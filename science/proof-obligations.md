# Decisive scientific checks

1. Fix a single operational definition of record readability, conditional total correlation, and system coherence, with the same assumptions in upper and lower bounds.
2. Derive the finite-N upper bound from a valid information-theoretic inequality. Check the sharp leading constant rather than relying on an unproved asymptotic expansion.
3. Give normalized conditional distributions and explicit purifications for a matching lower construction; calculate every marginal, correlation budget, and visibility.
4. Test whether all conditional record pairs can be exactly independent in a separate construction, and distinguish pairwise independence from bounded global total correlation.
5. Investigate the quantum noncommuting extension: measurement data processing may bound the measured affinity but need not automatically bound all proposed notions of recoverable coherence.
6. Compare the strongest existing correlation-budget bounds and quantum Darwinism counterexamples statement by statement; identify any prior result that already implies the claimed constant.
7. Separate unconstrained global coherent constructions from physically local, finite-depth, or dynamical generation.

**Stop condition:** if the sharp result fails in the stated unrestricted model, correct the claim before any publication or educational furnishing.
