# Closest-work review queue

Quantum Darwinism, spectrum broadcast structure, strong quantum Darwinism, and correlated environmental fragments provide the conceptual background. Pairwise independence alone is known not to imply global independence; this cannot be claimed as a discovery.

The crucial novelty test is whether earlier correlation-budget results already prove an N-dependent sharp visibility upper bound with the same assumptions and attainability. Check the primary sources, editions, and precise statements before claiming priority. Do not infer novelty from differing terminology.
